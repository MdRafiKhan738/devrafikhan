Musicians and creators were juggling a distribution service, a voice generator, a video tool, and a channel-ownership lookup across four different logins. Zinetic Music puts all of it - and the dashboard that ties it together - behind one account.

The Challenge

A creator's toolkit was scattered across a distribution service, a voice tool, a video editor, and a rights-lookup site.

Releasing a track worldwide, generating a clean voiceover, dubbing a video into another language, or checking who owns a YouTube channel each usually meant a different subscription and a different dashboard. Zinetic needed one account, one wallet, and one place to do all of it.

The Approach

Four studios, one dashboard - Music, AI Voice & Audio, AI Video, and Creator Tools, built end to end with Claude Code.

I built the platform as four connected studios under one account: unlimited worldwide music distribution with royalty reporting, an AI Music Generator, a six-tool voice and audio studio (generation, changing, cleaning, dubbing, sound effects, transcription), an eight-tool video studio (avatars, translation, lip sync, prompt-to-video, short-clip generation), and a Creator Tools studio for MCN/CMS channel-ownership lookups. Checkout runs through SSLCommerz so creators can pay with bKash, Nagad, Rocket, or card, with usage only counted on jobs that actually succeed.

The Result

17 services, 4 studios, one account - and artists keep up to 90% of their royalties.

Zinetic Music now runs unlimited worldwide music releases with up to 90% royalty share on its top plan, alongside on-demand AI voice, video, and creator tools - all metered and billed from the same dashboard instead of four separate tools.
